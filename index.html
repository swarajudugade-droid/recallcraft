What this MVP guarantees

  • Retrieval happens against the supplied textbook index, not a general web search.
  • AI mode is instructed to answer only from retrieved passages.
  • Source cards identify the textbook, chapter, PDF page and estimated printed page.
  • If retrieval finds no useful text, the tutor says that it cannot answer reliably.
  • Chapter summaries and revision notes select sentences from the indexed text; practice prompts are templates, not official exam questions.

Text extraction may omit or distort diagrams, tables, and image-based equations. Verify important answers in the original PDF.
